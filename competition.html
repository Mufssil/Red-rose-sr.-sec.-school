Inter-School Olympiad 2026

About Competition

The Inter-School Olympiad is a prestigious competition where students from various schools compete in academic and technical subjects to showcase their excellence.

Eligibility

All students from Class 6 to Class 12 are eligible. Students must be regular attendees with no disciplinary issues.

Rules

1. Each participant must register individually. 2. Registration fee required. 3. On-time appearance mandatory. 4. Fair conduct expected.

Last Date

Registration closes on August 15, 2026. Late registrations will not be accepted.

Prizes

1st Place: Cash Prize + Trophy | 2nd Place: Cash Prize + Certificate | 3rd Place: Certificate | All participants get participation medal.

Mathematics | Science | English | Hindi | Social Studies | General Knowledge | Technology
